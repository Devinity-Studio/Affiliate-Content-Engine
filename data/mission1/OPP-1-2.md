## Selection Rationale (not selected)

While the Shopee Affiliate Program terms (EV-1-2) establish general affiliate eligibility and EV-1-7 documents the condition that commissions are earned only when orders reach "สำเร็จ" status, these records do not map to the delivery platform ecosystems. The niche problem is well-characterized from a consumer perspective, but the candidate_offers list references programs whose terms, commission structures, and payout conditions were not inspected. Without verified program terms, any claims about earnings or viability would be unsupported UNKNOWN claims, violating AC-01's requirement that load-bearing claims be FACT/INFERENCE with sources. The opportunity remains plausible for a later cycle when delivery platform terms are inspected.
